Help Kynetix land on Google Play.

Before a new app goes public, Google wants 12 real people to test it for 14 days. Two taps from you and Kynetix gets there. It's free, there are no ads, and there's nothing to buy.

Two taps. In this order.

Grab your Android phone and use the Gmail you use on the Play Store.

STEP 1  ·  REQUIRED

Join the tester group first.

This is the one that matters. Google only lets members of this group into the test. Skip it and step 2 just says the app isn't available.

Join the tester group

Sign in if it asks, then tap Join group. You're in when the page says you're a member.

STEP 2

Become a tester.

Tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play. Kynetix installs like any other app.

Become a tester

Says it's not available? Either step 1 isn't done yet, or Google's still reviewing the test. Do step 1, then check back in a day.

THEN

Keep it for 14 days.

Leave it installed and open it now and then. Log a roti, start a workout, ask Kyno something. That's the whole job. Spotted something off? Tell Dhruv at kynetix.app@gmail.com.

Is this legit?

  • Both buttons go to Google's own sites: groups.google.com and play.google.com. Nothing installs from anywhere else.
  • The group sends no emails. Only Dhruv can see who joined.
  • Free. No ads, no payments, no card details, ever.
  • Your data stays yours. Read the privacy policy, and you can delete your account any time.
  • Want out later? Open the same Play testing page and tap Leave the program.

What's Kynetix?

A calorie, protein and workout tracker built for Indian food. Type meals the way you say them ("2 roti, 1 katori dal"), or snap your plate. Track every set and every PR. Ask Kyno, the coach that reads both your food and your training. Made in India by Dhruv Gupta. See what it does.

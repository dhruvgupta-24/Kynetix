How it works

  • Epley: 1RM = weight x (1 + reps / 30). Kynetix uses the same formula to spot personal records as you lift.
  • Training weights are your estimated 1RM times each percentage, rounded to the nearest 0.5 kg.

Kynetix works these out from your profile and adjusts them as you train and log. See the app.

Questions

How accurate is a one rep max calculator?

Close for sets of 3 to 10 reps, less so above 12. It estimates; it does not replace a careful test with a spotter.

What percentage of my 1RM should I train at?

Roughly 70 to 85% for most strength and muscle work, which lines up with sets of about 5 to 12 reps.

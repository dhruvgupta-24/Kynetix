Questions

Which exercises should a beginner start with?

A squat or leg press, a bench or dumbbell press, a row or lat pulldown, an overhead press and a Romanian deadlift cover most of the body. Add arms and calves once those feel steady.

How does Kynetix suggest the next weight?

From your own history on that lift: when you hit the top of the rep range on every set, it suggests the next jump; when you miss, it holds the weight.

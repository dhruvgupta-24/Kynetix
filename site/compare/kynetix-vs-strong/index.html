Kynetix vs Strong

Strong is a workout tracker. Kynetix tracks Indian food and gym training together, with an AI coach that reads both. Choose Strong if you only want a minimal set logger, or need an iPhone app today. Choose Kynetix if you also want to count Indian food and get coaching that connects what you eat with how you lift.

What Kynetix does

PlatformsAndroid (beta). iPhone is on the roadmap.
FocusFood and training in one app, built around Indian food.
Food loggingType meals in plain words ("2 roti, dal"); katori and roti portions; barcode scanning.
Workout loggingSets, reps, rest timer, splits, personal records and next-weight suggestions.
CoachKyno, an AI coach that reads your meals, lifts, steps and weight.

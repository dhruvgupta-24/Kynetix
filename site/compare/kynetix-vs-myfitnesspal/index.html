Kynetix vs MyFitnessPal

MyFitnessPal is a global calorie counter with a vast international food database. Kynetix is an offline-first, open-source nutrition and workout tracker built specifically for Indian food and strength training. Choose MyFitnessPal if you eat primarily packaged Western food and need an iOS app today. Choose Kynetix if you eat home-cooked Indian meals (dal, roti, sabzi), lift weights, want free barcode scanning with zero ads, and value private local data storage.

Which one should you pick?

  • Pick MyFitnessPal if: You consume mostly Western packaged groceries, travel internationally, or require an iPhone app right now.
  • Pick Kynetix if: You eat home-cooked Indian meals, lift weights regularly, refuse to pay subscription paywalls for barcode scanning, and want an ad-free, offline tracker that respects your privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kynetix better than MyFitnessPal?

It depends on your routine. If you eat home-cooked Indian meals, train with weights, and want an ad-free app with free barcode scanning and offline speed, Kynetix is tailored for you. If you eat international packaged foods or use an iPhone, MyFitnessPal offers a larger global database.

Does Kynetix charge for barcode scanning like MyFitnessPal?

No. Barcode scanning in Kynetix is completely free and powered by Open Food Facts, without requiring any paid subscription.

Can I use Kynetix and MyFitnessPal together?

Yes. Many people try a new app alongside their existing tracker. Kynetix reads steps, weight, and sleep directly from Android Health Connect, so it syncs seamlessly alongside other health tools.

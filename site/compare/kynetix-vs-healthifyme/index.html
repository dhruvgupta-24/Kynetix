Kynetix vs HealthifyMe

HealthifyMe is a commercial Indian diet and fitness platform designed around human coaches and weight loss programs. Kynetix is an independent, MIT-licensed open-source tracker built specifically for Indian lifters and home cooking. Choose HealthifyMe if you want paid personalized human coaching sessions or an iPhone app today. Choose Kynetix if you lift weights, want zero ads and zero tele-sales coaching calls, need full offline tracking, and prefer an open-source tool that keeps your health data private.

Which one should you pick?

  • Pick HealthifyMe if: You want a guided weight loss plan with human dietitians, or you currently need an iPhone app.
  • Pick Kynetix if: You train with weights, track progressive overload alongside your meals, want a clean interface with zero coaching phone calls or banner ads, and require fast offline logging.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kynetix better than HealthifyMe?

It depends on what you want out of your tracking. HealthifyMe is geared toward general weight loss with human nutritionists. Kynetix is purpose-built for lifters and self-directed tracking: it combines Indian food logging with complete gym workout routines, zero ads, and offline-first speed.

Does Kynetix call or message you to sell coaching plans?

Never. Kynetix is an independent open-source project with zero commercial sales teams, zero tele-calling, and zero in-app sales popups.

Can I use Kynetix and HealthifyMe together?

Yes. Kynetix reads steps, weight, and sleep directly from Android Health Connect, so it can run alongside any other health app without data conflicts.

High-protein vegetarian Indian diet

By Dhruv Gupta, maker of Kynetix. 20 October 2026. 4 min read.

A vegetarian can reach 100 to 140 g of protein a day on Indian food by building every meal around one dense source: paneer, soya chunks, curd or hung curd, tofu, milk or whey, then adding dal and chana on top. 50 g of dry soya chunks alone is about 26 g of protein; 100 g of paneer is about 18 g.

Why dal alone won't get you there

Dal is a good food, but it is not a high-protein one by the katori. A katori of dal tadka is about 6.8 g protein for 158 kcal. To reach 120 g from dal you would eat around twenty katoris. Dal is the support act; the lead needs to be denser.

A sample 120 g day

  • Breakfast: 2 moong dal cheelas (13 g) and a glass of toned milk (8 g).
  • Lunch: 2 roti, a katori of soya chunk curry (18 g) and a katori of curd (5 g).
  • Evening: 40 g roasted chana (8 g) or a scoop of whey (24 g).
  • Dinner: rice or roti with paneer bhurji (18.8 g) and dal (5.7 g).

That is around 110 to 130 g depending on the snack, from ordinary Indian food. The trick is that every meal has one dense source.

Make it easy to keep

  • Cook soya chunks in batches: boil, squeeze, freeze. They take the flavour of whatever gravy you put them in.
  • Hang curd overnight in a cloth to double its protein per spoon.
  • Keep roasted chana and a protein source at your desk, not just at home.
  • Spread protein over three or four meals instead of one huge dinner.

What about B12 and iron?

Vegetarian diets can run low on vitamin B12 and sometimes iron. Kynetix tracks iron, calcium, B12 and a few other nutrients from its micronutrient table, so you can see the trend. If you suspect a deficiency, a blood test and a doctor beat any app.

Protein per calorie, the number that matters on a cut

When you are losing fat, the useful question is how much protein a food gives for its calories. By that measure, the leaders in Kynetix's table are whey, soya chunks, low-fat paneer, and hung curd or Greek yogurt. Regular paneer, peanuts and chana are good foods but bring a lot of fat or carbs along with the protein.

This doesn't mean avoiding paneer or peanuts. It means planning around them: a katori of paneer bhurji at dinner is a fine choice, while snacking on handfuls of peanuts all evening can quietly eat your deficit.

Combining proteins

Grains and pulses complement each other, which is why dal chawal, rajma chawal and idli sambar have lasted so long. You don't need to combine them in the same meal; eating a variety across the day is enough. Dairy, soya and whey are complete proteins on their own.

Budget-friendly protein

  • Soya chunks are usually the cheapest protein per gram in an Indian kitchen.
  • Milk and curd from a local dairy are both affordable and versatile.
  • Chana, rajma and moong in bulk, cooked in a pressure cooker, last the week.
  • Whey is expensive per kilo but cheap per gram of protein compared with many foods.

Common mistakes

  • Counting dal as the main protein. It helps, but it is the support act.
  • One huge protein meal at night instead of spreading it out.
  • Assuming all paneer is the same. Check the pack: low-fat paneer has noticeably more protein per calorie.
  • Forgetting that sauces and gravies add calories, not protein.

Key takeaways

  • Put one dense source in every meal: soya, paneer, tofu, hung curd, milk or whey.
  • Treat dal and chana as support, not the main source.
  • On a cut, prefer low-fat paneer, soya, tofu and hung curd for protein per calorie.
  • Batch-cook soya and hang curd so the easy choice is the high-protein one.

Questions

How can a vegetarian get 100 g of protein a day in India?

Build each meal around one dense source (soya chunks, paneer, tofu, hung curd, milk or whey) and add dal or chana. 50 g of dry soya chunks alone is about 26 g.

Which Indian vegetarian food has the most protein?

Per serving in Kynetix's table, soya chunks (26 g per 50 g dry), whey (24 g per scoop) and low-fat paneer (24 g per 100 g) lead.

Veg diet mein protein kaise badhayein?

Har meal mein ek strong source rakho: paneer, soya, dahi, tofu ya whey. Saath mein dal aur chana. 100 g paneer mein lagbhag 18 g protein hota hai.

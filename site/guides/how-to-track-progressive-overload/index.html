How to track progressive overload

By Dhruv Gupta, maker of Kynetix. 20 October 2026. 4 min read.

Progressive overload means doing a little more over time: more reps at the same weight, then more weight. The simplest way to track it is double progression: work in a rep range, add reps each session, and add the smallest weight jump once every set hits the top of the range.

Double progression, step by step

  • Pick a rep range, for example 8 to 12.
  • Choose a weight you can lift for 8 clean reps on every set.
  • Each session, try to add a rep to some sets.
  • When every set reaches 12, add the smallest jump: 2.5 kg on a barbell, the next dumbbell, the next pin.
  • Drop back near 8 reps with the new weight and climb again.

It works because you always know exactly what to beat.

What to write down

For every working set: the weight and the reps. That's it. Warm-up sets don't count toward your progress, so keep them separate. Notes on how a set felt help when you look back.

Estimated one rep max

An estimated one rep max (1RM) turns any set into one comparable number. Kynetix uses the Epley formula: weight x (1 + reps / 30). 60 kg for 8 reps estimates to 76 kg. If that number rises over weeks, you're getting stronger, even if you never test a true max. Try the one rep max calculator.

Volume

Volume is sets x reps x weight. It is a useful check for muscle growth: rising volume on a muscle over a few weeks usually means you're doing enough. Kynetix totals it for each session and shows kilos lifted for the week.

When progress stalls

  • Check sleep and food first. Eating below maintenance makes progress slower, especially on big lifts.
  • Repeat the weight for another week before you change anything.
  • Take a lighter week if several lifts stall at once.
  • Swap a stuck exercise for a close variation for a few weeks.

Progressive overload is not only adding weight

  • More reps at the same weight.
  • More sets for a muscle across the week.
  • The same weight with cleaner, slower reps.
  • Shorter rest at the same weight and reps.
  • A harder variation of the same movement.

Weight and reps are the easiest to measure, which is why they are the default. The others are useful when the weight jump is too big, for example on small dumbbell moves.

A worked example

Say your plan is 3 sets of 8 to 12 on the lat pulldown. Week one: 45 kg for 10, 9 and 8. Week two: 10, 10 and 9. Week three: 12, 11, 10. Week four: 12, 12, 12. Now add the next pin, perhaps 50 kg, and you might get 9, 8, 8. The cycle starts again. On paper it is slow; over a year it adds up to real strength and muscle.

Why a log beats memory

Most people walk into the gym and lift what feels right. Without a log you repeat the same weights for months. With a log, each session has a target: beat last time by one rep. That small target is what progressive overload looks like in practice.

Deload weeks

Every six to ten weeks, or when several lifts stall and you feel beaten up, take a lighter week: the same exercises at about 60 to 70% of your usual weight, or fewer sets. You usually come back stronger. It is a tool, not a sign of weakness.

Key takeaways

  • Work in a rep range and beat last session by a rep where you can.
  • Add the smallest weight jump once every set hits the top of the range.
  • Use estimated one rep max and weekly volume as trend lines, not daily scores.
  • Log every working set; memory always rounds up.

A note on form

Progress only counts if the reps look the same. If a new personal record comes with a shorter range of motion or a bounce, it isn't really more strength. Keep the same depth and tempo, and add weight only when the reps stay clean.

Questions

What is progressive overload?

Gradually doing more over time: more reps at the same weight, then more weight, so your muscles keep a reason to adapt.

How do I know when to increase weight?

When every working set reaches the top of your rep range with good form, add the smallest jump and start again near the bottom of the range.

Weight kab badhana chahiye?

Jab har set mein rep range ka top number clean form ke saath ho jaye, tab sabse chhota jump add karo.

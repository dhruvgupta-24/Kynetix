Gym workout split for beginners

By Dhruv Gupta, maker of Kynetix. 20 October 2026. 4 min read.

If you can train three days a week, start with full body. Four days suits an upper and lower split. Five or six days suits push, pull, legs. A bro split (one muscle a day) works too, but hits each muscle only once a week, which is slower for most beginners.

Choose by the days you can actually train

Days a weekSplitEach muscle trained
3Full body3 times a week
4Upper / lower2 times a week
5 to 6Push / pull / legsAbout 2 times a week
5Bro splitOnce a week

The best split is the one you will still be doing in six months. Pick the days you can protect, not the days you hope for.

Full body (3 days)

  • Squat or leg press: 3 x 6 to 10
  • Bench press or incline dumbbell press: 3 x 6 to 10
  • Lat pulldown or seated cable row: 3 x 8 to 12
  • Romanian deadlift: 2 x 8 to 10
  • Lateral raise and an arm move: 2 x 12 to 15

Push / pull / legs

  • Push: bench press, overhead press, incline dumbbell press, lateral raise, tricep pushdown.
  • Pull: pull-ups or lat pulldown, bent-over row, face pull, biceps curl, hammer curl.
  • Legs: squat, Romanian deadlift, leg press, leg curl, calf raise.

Run it once (3 days) or twice (6 days) a week.

Bro split

Chest, back, shoulders, arms, legs, one per day. It is popular in Indian gyms and it does work, especially for people who love long sessions on one muscle. The catch is frequency: each muscle grows from a session and then waits a week. If you choose it, keep the volume high and the effort honest.

Sets, reps and rest

  • Big compound lifts: 3 to 4 sets of 5 to 10 reps, 2 to 3 minutes rest.
  • Smaller moves: 2 to 3 sets of 10 to 15 reps, about 90 seconds rest.
  • Finish most sets with one or two good reps left in the tank.

A sample upper and lower week

  • Day 1, upper: bench press, lat pulldown, overhead press, seated cable row, curls and pushdowns.
  • Day 2, lower: squat, Romanian deadlift, leg press, leg curl, calf raise.
  • Day 3: rest or a walk.
  • Day 4, upper: incline dumbbell press, pull-ups or pulldown, lateral raise, row, arms.
  • Day 5, lower: leg press or front squat, hip thrust, leg extension, leg curl, calves.

How long should a session be?

Forty five to seventy five minutes is plenty for most beginners. If your sessions run past ninety minutes, you're probably resting too long between small exercises or doing too many of them. Kynetix shows how long the planned session will take before you start, so you can trim it on busy days.

When to change your split

Stick with one split for at least eight to twelve weeks. Changing every month makes it hard to see whether you are progressing. Change it when your schedule changes, when you have stopped progressing for several weeks despite sleeping and eating well, or simply when you've lost interest. Enjoying the plan is a real reason; it keeps you coming back.

Warm-ups and safety

Before each big lift, do two or three lighter warm-up sets, building up to your working weight. Kynetix keeps warm-up sets separate so they don't count as your progress. Learn the movement with a weight you control, and ask a trainer to watch your squat and deadlift form early on.

Myths you can ignore

  • "You must train six days to grow." Three good sessions a week build muscle well for beginners.
  • "Muscle confusion." Changing exercises every week makes progress harder to track, not faster.
  • "Cardio kills gains." A sensible amount of walking or cardio is good for your heart and barely touches strength gains.

Key takeaways

  • Choose the split by how many days you can really train.
  • Train each muscle about twice a week if you can.
  • Stick with one plan for eight to twelve weeks and log every working set.
  • Use progressive overload; the split only organises the work.

Questions

What is the best workout split for beginners?

Full body three days a week for most beginners; upper lower for four days; push pull legs for five or six.

Is bro split good for beginners?

It works, but each muscle is trained once a week. Most beginners progress faster training each muscle twice a week.

Beginner ke liye kaunsa split best hai?

Hafte mein 3 din ho to full body, 4 din ho to upper lower, 5 se 6 din ho to push pull legs.

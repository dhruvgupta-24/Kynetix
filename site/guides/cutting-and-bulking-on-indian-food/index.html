Cutting and bulking on Indian food

By Dhruv Gupta, maker of Kynetix. 20 October 2026. 4 min read.

To cut, eat about 22% below maintenance with high protein (around 2.1 g per kg) and keep lifting heavy. To bulk, eat about 8 to 10% above maintenance with around 1.8 g per kg of protein. On Indian food, the levers are roti and rice portions, oil and ghee, and making sure every meal has a protein source.

Set the numbers first

Find your maintenance with the calorie calculator. Kynetix cuts at 22% below maintenance (350 to 550 kcal), lean bulks at 8% above (150 to 250 kcal) and bulks at 10% (200 to 350 kcal). Protein comes from the protein calculator.

Cutting: what changes on the plate

  • Keep the roti count steady and drop one serving, or swap a bowl of rice for two roti.
  • Choose the leaner curry: plain dal (112 kcal a katori) over dal makhani (210 kcal).
  • Swap regular paneer (295 kcal per 100 g) for low-fat paneer (200 kcal) or tofu.
  • Measure the oil. Each teaspoon is about 45 kcal.
  • Add a protein snack: roasted chana, hung curd or whey.

Bulking: what changes on the plate

  • Add a roti or half a bowl of rice to each meal before reaching for junk.
  • Milk is an easy surplus: a glass of toned milk is about 150 kcal and 8 g protein.
  • Peanuts (170 kcal per 30 g) and peanut butter add calories without a huge meal.
  • Keep the surplus small. Bigger surpluses mostly add fat.

Training doesn't change

Cut or bulk, keep lifting with progressive overload. On a cut, holding your strength is the win. On a bulk, adding reps and weight every few weeks tells you the surplus is doing its job.

Training days and rest days

Kynetix gives training days a little more food than rest days (140 to 220 kcal apart), weighted so your weekly average still matches the goal. It means more fuel when you lift without overshooting the week.

How long should a cut or bulk last?

A cut usually runs 8 to 16 weeks, depending on how much fat you want to lose. A bulk works best over several months, because muscle grows slowly. Between them, a few weeks at maintenance helps your appetite, energy and lifts settle before the next phase.

A sample cutting day on Indian food

  • Breakfast: 2 moong dal cheelas with curd.
  • Lunch: 2 roti, dal and a dry sabzi, with a side of grilled chicken or paneer.
  • Snack: roasted chana or a scoop of whey.
  • Dinner: a bowl of rice or 2 roti with chicken curry or soya curry, and a big salad.

A sample bulking day

  • Breakfast: oats with milk and a banana, plus eggs or paneer.
  • Lunch: 3 roti, rajma or chole, rice and curd.
  • Snack: a glass of milk with peanut butter toast.
  • Dinner: rice, dal and chicken, fish or paneer, with ghee on the rice if you're struggling to eat enough.

Signs you need to adjust

  • On a cut, no weight change for two to three weeks: trim another 100 to 150 kcal or add steps.
  • On a cut, lifts dropping fast and feeling drained: move closer to maintenance for a week.
  • On a bulk, weight jumping more than about half a kilo a week: trim the surplus.
  • On a bulk, no weight change for a month: add a roti or a glass of milk a day.

Myths you can ignore

  • "You must cut out roti and rice to lose fat." You need a calorie deficit; how you build it is up to you.
  • "Dirty bulking is faster." It adds fat faster, which you later have to cut.
  • "Ghee is a superfood for bulking." It is a useful calorie source, and that is all.

Key takeaways

  • Cut at about 22% below maintenance with high protein; bulk at about 8 to 10% above.
  • Change portions of roti, rice, oil and ghee before changing what you eat.
  • Keep a protein source in every meal either way.
  • Judge progress by the weekly weight average and your lifts.

Check the trend, not the day

Weigh yourself a few mornings a week and watch the weekly average. Aim for 0.5 to 1% of body weight a week on a cut, and a slow climb on a bulk. Kynetix reads weight from Health Connect if you allow it.

Questions

How many calories should I cut to lose fat?

Kynetix uses about 22% below maintenance, between 350 and 550 kcal a day, which usually means losing 0.5 to 1% of body weight a week.

How do I bulk on Indian food?

Eat about 8 to 10% above maintenance: add roti or rice, milk, peanuts and an extra protein source, and keep training with progressive overload.

Cutting mein kya khana chahiye?

Protein zyada rakho (paneer, soya, dahi, chicken, eggs), tel aur ghee naap ke, aur roti ya chawal thoda kam.

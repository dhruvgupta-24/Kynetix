See how it works

Food

Eat like you eat

Type it the way you'd say it. Two roti, dal, a bowl of aloo sabzi, curd on the side. Kyno works out the calories and protein, and calls it Kyno's estimate until you say it's right.

kDescribe it2 roti, dal makhani, half bowl rice

Packaged? Scan the barcode and log the exact pack. Fix a portion once and Kynetix remembers how you eat it.

Training

Every set counts

Big numbers, quick steppers and your last set right there. Log it in a tap and the rest timer takes over.

Kyno suggests the next weight from your own history. Hit a personal record and you hear about it on the spot. Gravity filed a complaint.

Your week

Your split, your week

Push, pull, legs, a bro split or your own thing. Pick a split and Kynetix lays out the week: what's on today, how long it runs, and what you've moved so far.

Kilos lifted, sets, streak and records, without a spreadsheet in sight.

Targets

Targets that know leg day

Your calorie and protein targets come from your body, your goal and your training. Train days eat a little more. Rest days a little less.

Connect Health Connect and Kynetix reads four things to keep the numbers honest. Read only. It never writes.

Want them without opening the app? Put the rings on your home screen with a widget.

Kyno

Meet Kyno

Kyno is the coach inside Kynetix. It has already read your last two weeks of meals, lifts, steps and weight, so you can skip the backstory and just ask.

Hover a questionTap a question to see how Kyno answers.

  • kKyno

    You've got 1,901 kcal and 117 g of protein left. Paneer bhurji with 2 roti for lunch, curd and a banana at 5, and dal chawal with chicken for dinner gets you close. I skipped anything you've already had today.

  • kKyno

    Yes, and not subtly. The days you logged average 611 kcal and 27 g of protein, against 2,459 and 144. Either you're under-eating or under-logging. Log every meal this week and I'll tell you which.

  • kKyno

    Tomorrow is Back + Biceps, so you're on the training day target. Poha and a whey shake before, rajma chawal with curd after, paneer and roti at night. Most of the carbs land around the session, where they do some work.

  • kKyno

    Sleep has been under 6.5 hours three nights running, and your rows have sat at 40 kg for two sessions. Keep the weight tomorrow, drop one set, and get to bed earlier. That's a pattern in your logs, not a diagnosis.

  • kKyno

    Your whey costs more per gram of protein than soya chunks. 50 g of dry soya is about 26 g of protein for a few rupees. Stir it into the rajma and nobody at the table will notice.

Example replies. The real ones come from your own logs. Snap a menu or your plate and Kyno reads it too. Already pay for ChatGPT? Connect your account in Settings and Kyno runs on it. Kyno's numbers are estimates, and Kyno is not a doctor. If a reply is off, report it right from the reply.

Share cards

Share the damage

Finish a session and Kynetix tallies it up: new bests, kilos moved, the lot. Then turn it into a card. Five styles, story or post size, on graphite, paper or your own photo.

Your data

No funny business

Your meals and lifts are yours. Here's the deal in plain words.

  • No adsNone. Not now, not as a “free tier”.
  • We don't sell your dataNot to advertisers, not to anyone.
  • No third-party trackersNo analytics or ad SDKs in the app. Error reports go to our own database, scrubbed first.
  • Health Connect is read onlySteps, weight, sleep and HRV, only if you allow it. Kynetix never writes to it.
  • AI only when you askMeal text, photos and questions go to an AI provider only when you use Kyno or an estimate. Photos aren't stored.
  • Delete means deleteOne screen in the app wipes your account and everything in it, straight away.

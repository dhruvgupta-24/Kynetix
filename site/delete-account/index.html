Delete your account

You can delete your Kynetix account and all of its data at any time, in the app or by email. Kynetix is an Android fitness and nutrition app by Dhruv Gupta.

On this page
  1. In the app
  2. By email
  3. What gets deleted
  4. What we keep

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kynetix and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture or initials at the top of the home screen to open your profile and settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account and all its data are deleted straight away, and you're signed out.

No app? Ask by email

Send an email from the email address you use to sign in to Kynetix, so we can tell it's you:

To
kynetix.app@gmail.com
Subject
Delete my Kynetix account
Write the email

We may reply to confirm it's you. We delete the account within 30 days of your request, usually much sooner, and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account:

  • Your sign-in and account
  • Your profile, body details, goal and targets
  • Every meal, macro, water and weight entry you logged
  • Workouts, splits, sets, records, achievements and insights
  • Saved meals, quick-adds and the food memory built from your edits
  • Your profile photo
  • Your ChatGPT connection and its tokens
  • Error reports and any Kyno replies you reported

Deletion can't be undone.

What we keep

Nothing in our live database. Copies in our hosting provider's backups and short-lived server logs are purged within 30 days. Kyno chat history and the photos you send to Kyno are never stored by us, so there's nothing to delete there.

Want to remove data without deleting your account? Most of it can be edited or deleted in the app, or email kynetix.app@gmail.com. Full details are in the privacy policy.

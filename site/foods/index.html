Questions

Where do these numbers come from?

From the same food tables the Kynetix app uses: cooked Indian dishes per 100 g worked from IFCT 2017 and USDA FoodData Central for typical home recipes, plus everyday foods per serving. They are estimates, and your oil, ghee and portions change the real number.

How big is a katori in Kynetix?

150 g. With roti or rice, Kynetix sizes the curry by how much you eat with each roti or ladle of rice instead.

Roti calories kitni hoti hain?

Ek plain roti mein lagbhag 90 kcal aur 3 g protein hota hai, Kynetix ki food table ke hisaab se.

All values are estimates for typical home recipes. Kynetix lets you correct any number, and remembers your version.

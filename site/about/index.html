What is Kynetix

Kynetix is an Android app for calorie, protein and workout tracking, built around Indian food. You log meals in plain words like "2 roti and a katori of dal", log your sets and personal records, and ask Kyno, an AI coach that reads both. It is free during early access, has no ads, and is made in India by Dhruv Gupta.

Use this description as is, or the short one: Kynetix is a calorie and workout tracker for Indian food, with an AI coach called Kyno.

What it does

  • Food logging in plain words. Type "2 roti, 1 katori dal, aloo sabzi" and Kynetix estimates calories and protein. Indian portions like katori, roti, bowl and plate are understood, and it remembers your corrections.
  • Barcode scanning for packaged food, using Open Food Facts.
  • Targets that follow your training. Calorie and protein targets come from your body and goal, a little higher on training days.
  • Workout logging. Splits, sets, reps, rest timers and automatic personal record detection.
  • Kyno, the AI coach. Reads your meals, lifts, steps and weight and answers questions like what to eat to hit your protein. Its numbers are estimates and it is not a doctor.
  • Health Connect. Reads steps, weight, sleep and HRV with your permission. Read only.
  • Home screen widgets in four sizes: 2x2, 4x2, 2x4 and 4x4.
  • Share cards. Turn a finished session into a Poster, Receipt, Skyline, In real terms or Sticker card.

More on the home page, in the questions and answers, and in the privacy policy.

Who makes it

Kynetix is designed and built by Dhruv Gupta, an independent developer in India, for people who eat dal and roti and lift four days a week. There is no company behind it and no investors. Feedback goes straight to him, from inside the app under Profile > Talk to us, or by email.

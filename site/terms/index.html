1. Who can use Kynetix

You need to be 18 or older. One account per person, and keep your sign-in to yourself.

2. Not medical advice

Kynetix and Kyno help you track food and training. They don't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Calorie, protein and training numbers are estimates. Talk to a qualified doctor or dietitian before big changes to your diet or training, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating. Stop and get help if something hurts.

3. AI can be wrong

Kyno and the meal estimates are produced by AI models. They can misread a photo, guess a portion wrong or give advice that doesn't fit you. Check anything that matters. Your own edits always win. If a reply is harmful or plain wrong, report it from the reply in the app.

# Kynetix voice

Kynetix talks like your gym buddy who knows nutrition: chill, confident, a little playful, and never preachy. It's written for Indian gym-goers who eat roti, dal and paneer and lift four days a week.

## The rules
1. **Short and casual.** Use contractions and plain words. Example: "Let's get you set up," not "Please complete the following steps."
2. **Chill, not cringe.** One light touch of personality per screen is plenty. No slang pile-ups, no "fam", no emoji in buttons or errors, and no exclamation marks in errors.
3. **Name the outcome.** Buttons say exactly what happens: "Log meal", "Start workout", "Connect ChatGPT", "Skip for now". The toast or result uses the same verb: "Meal logged."
4. **Sentence case** everywhere. Use no ALL CAPS, except strings that tests pin.
5. **Honest numbers.** AI numbers are "Kyno's estimate" until you confirm them. Never claim something was saved, synced or done before it actually was.
6. **Errors say what happened and what to do, calmly.** No apologies, no blame, no raw exception text. Example: "Couldn't reach the server. Check your connection and try again."
7. **Encourage without guilt.** "Back at it. Even a light session counts." Never "You failed your goal."
8. **Indian context, naturally.** Use roti, dal, paneer, "kg" and "kcal" where relevant. Don't force Hinglish; at most one light word occasionally ("Chalo, let's go"), and never in errors or legal copy.
9. **No AI hype.** No "AI-powered", "revolutionary" or "unlock your potential". Kyno is just Kyno.
10. **Legal, privacy and health copy stays plain and precise.** The chill voice never softens a consent, privacy or safety message.

## Patterns

| Moment | Write like this |
|---|---|
| Welcome | "Real food. Real lifts. Let's get you set up." |
| Sign in | "Welcome back." · Button: "Sign in" |
| Sign up | "Make it official." · Button: "Create account" |
| Password field | Hint: "At least 8 characters" · Toggle: "Show" / "Hide" |
| Onboarding step | Title: "How tall are you?" · Helper: "Helps us size your targets." |
| Optional step | Primary: "Connect ChatGPT" · Secondary: "Skip for now" · Note: "You can do this later in Settings." |
| Loading | "Crunching your macros…", "Warming up…", "Pulling your history…" |
| Empty state | "Nothing logged yet. Your first meal sets the tone." · Action: "Log a meal" |
| Success | "Meal logged." / "Workout saved. Solid session." |
| Error | "That didn't go through. Try again in a moment." |
| Over target | "You're 180 kcal over. No stress, tomorrow's a fresh page." |
| Streak | "4 days running. Keep it rolling." |

## Loaders
Loader copy must match what's actually happening, and it rotates no faster than every 2.5s. Never show fake progress percentages.

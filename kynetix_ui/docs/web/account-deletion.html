Delete your Kynetix account

Kynetix is a fitness and nutrition app. You can delete your account and all its data at any time.

Fastest way: in the app

  1. Open Kynetix and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.
